Friday, November 6, 2026 — Dubai · Paris Masters Quarterfinals

Alcaraz Saves Two Match Points to Force a Decider Against Fritz in Paris

Ben Shelton [3] beat fellow American Tommy Paul [12] 6-4, 4-6, 6-3, and France's Luca Van Assche beat Australian Rinky Hijikata 6-4, 6-7(4), 7-5, to reach the semifinals. The Alcaraz–Fritz quarterfinal is live on Court Central. Fritz won the first-set tiebreak 12-10 (7-6), Alcaraz saved two match points in the second set and took it 7-5, and the match is now in a deciding third set. Alcaraz reached this stage without hitting a ball in the round of 16 after Matteo Berrettini withdrew with arm pain, while Fritz beat Arthur Fils 6-3, 6-4 and needs a win to keep his ATP Finals hopes alive.

Paris Masters Storylines

QF · LIVE

Alcaraz Saves Two Match Points to Force a Deciding Set

Taylor Fritz won the first-set tiebreak 12-10 to take it 7-6(10) and had match points in the second set. Carlos Alcaraz saved two of them and went on to win the set 7-5, sending the quarterfinal to a deciding third set with a semifinal place on the line. Fritz needs the win to keep his ATP Finals hopes alive.

FINAL · QF

Van Assche Outlasts Hijikata in Three Sets

Luca Van Assche took the first set 6-4, Rinky Hijikata won a second-set tiebreak 7-4 to level, and Van Assche closed out the decider 7-5 to reach the semifinals.

FINAL · QF

Shelton Edges Paul in Three Sets to Reach the Semifinals

Seeded third, Ben Shelton beat compatriot Tommy Paul [12] 6-4, 4-6, 6-3 on Court Central. Shelton took the first set 6-4, Paul levelled with a 6-4 second, and Shelton closed out the decider 6-3 to move into the last four.

QF · TONIGHT

Alcaraz Rested, Fritz Fighting for the ATP Finals

Alcaraz comes in fresh after the Berrettini walkover. Fritz has to keep winning to stay in the ATP Finals race, and he has already beaten home favourite Arthur Fils in straight sets this week. Most previews make Alcaraz the favourite.

R16

Fritz Beats Fils 6-3, 6-4 to Reach the Last Eight

The match was moved to a 7 p.m. slot because of what it meant for the ATP Finals race. Fritz took it in straight sets.

R16

Berrettini Withdraws With Arm Pain, Alcaraz Advances

Alcaraz moved into the quarterfinals without playing the round of 16 after Matteo Berrettini pulled out.
