Alcaraz Meets Fritz in the Paris Quarterfinals

The Alcaraz–Fritz quarterfinal is confirmed for Friday night on Court Central, not before 7 p.m. Paris time. Alcaraz reached this stage without hitting a ball in the round of 16 after Matteo Berrettini withdrew with arm pain, while Fritz beat Arthur Fils 6-3, 6-4 and needs a win to keep his ATP Finals hopes alive.
