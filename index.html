Tuesday, November 10, 2026 — Dubai · WTA Finals, Indian Wells

Andreeva Beats Svitolina 6-4, 6-0 on Day Two of the WTA Finals; Sabalenka Beats Muchová 7-6(2), 7-5

The season-ending WTA Finals run November 8–15 at Indian Wells. On Tuesday, November 10, Mirra Andreeva beat Elina Svitolina 6-4, 6-0 and Aryna Sabalenka beat Karolina Muchová 7-6(2), 7-5. On Monday, November 9, Linda Noskova beat Marta Kostyuk 6-2, 6-4 and Coco Gauff beat Jessica Pegula 6-4, 6-7(5), 6-2. Wednesday, November 11 brings Marta Kostyuk against Jessica Pegula at 12:00 AM and Coco Gauff against Linda Noskova at about 1:10 AM, both on Court 2. Thursday, November 12 has Elina Svitolina against Karolína Muchová at 12:00 AM and Aryna Sabalenka against Mirra Andreeva at about 1:10 AM. Friday, November 13 brings Marta Kostyuk against Coco Gauff at about 12:00 AM and Jessica Pegula against Linda Noskova at about 1:10 AM.

WTA Finals Storylines

WITHDRAWAL

World Number One Rybakina Withdraws From the WTA Finals

World number one Elena Rybakina has withdrawn from the WTA Finals because of a viral illness, BBC Sport reported on 7 November 2026. Rybakina went undefeated on her way to winning the WTA Finals for the first time in 2025.

Source: BBC Sport, “World number one Rybakina withdraws from WTA Finals”, 7 November 2026.

FINAL · OPENING MATCH

Noskova Beats Kostyuk 6-2, 6-4 in the WTA Finals Opener

Linda Noskova won the opening group-stage match on Court 2, beating Marta Kostyuk 6-2, 6-4.

FINAL · ALL-AMERICAN

Gauff Beats Pegula in Three Sets

Coco Gauff beat Jessica Pegula 6-4, 6-7(5), 6-2 in their group-stage match on Court 2. Gauff won the first set 6-4, Pegula took the second in a tiebreak, and Gauff closed out the decider 6-2.

FINAL · DAY TWO

Sabalenka Beats Muchová in Two Tight Sets

Aryna Sabalenka beat Karolina Muchová 7-6(2), 7-5 in the opening match of Tuesday’s group-stage schedule on Court 2, taking the first set in a tiebreak and the second 7-5.

FINAL · DAY TWO

Andreeva Beats Svitolina 6-4, 6-0 in 71 Minutes

Mirra Andreeva beat Elina Svitolina 6-4, 6-0 on Court 2, taking the first set 6-4 and then winning the second without losing a game. The win puts Andreeva 1–0 in Group 2.

FINAL · DAY THREE

Pegula Beats Kostyuk 6-2, 7-5 to Level Her Group Record

Jessica Pegula beat Marta Kostyuk 6-2, 7-5 on Court 2, bouncing back from her opening loss to Coco Gauff. Kostyuk, an alternate, is 0–2 after also losing to Linda Noskova on Monday, and is eliminated from the round robin.

FINAL · DAY THREE

Noskova Beats Gauff 6-3, 6-3 to Go 2–0 in Group 1

Linda Noskova beat Coco Gauff 6-3, 6-3 on Court 2, adding to her opening win over Marta Kostyuk and moving to 2–0 in Group 1. Gauff is 1–1 after beating Jessica Pegula on Monday.

THURSDAY · NOV 12

Svitolina and Muchová Look for a First Group 2 Win

Elina Svitolina and Karolína Muchová, who both lost their opening matches, meet on Court 2 at 12:00 AM UAE on Thursday, November 12.

THURSDAY · NOV 12

Sabalenka Meets Andreeva With Group 2 Leadership at Stake

Aryna Sabalenka and Mirra Andreeva, both 1–0 in Group 2, follow on Court 2 at an estimated 1:10 AM UAE on Thursday, November 12.

FRIDAY · NOV 13

Kostyuk and Gauff Meet in Group 1

Marta Kostyuk, already eliminated at 0–2, plays Coco Gauff on Court 2 at an estimated 12:00 AM UAE on Friday, November 13.

FRIDAY · NOV 13

Pegula Faces Group Leader Noskova

Jessica Pegula plays Group 1 leader Linda Noskova on Court 2 at an estimated 1:10 AM UAE on Friday, November 13.
