Sunday, November 8, 2026 — Dubai · WTA Finals, Indian Wells

Gauff Beats Pegula in Three Sets and Noskova Beats Kostyuk on Opening Day of the WTA Finals

The season-ending WTA Finals run November 8–15 at Indian Wells. The group stage is under way in Dubai time on Monday, November 9, with Linda Noskova beating Marta Kostyuk 6-2, 6-4 on Court 2. Coco Gauff then beat fellow American Jessica Pegula 6-4, 6-7(5), 6-2 on Court 2. Tuesday, November 10 brings Aryna Sabalenka against Karolina Muchová at 12:00 AM and Mirra Andreeva against Elina Svitolina at about 1:10 AM, also on Court 2.

WTA Finals Storylines

WITHDRAWAL

World Number One Rybakina Withdraws From the WTA Finals

World number one Elena Rybakina has withdrawn from the WTA Finals because of a viral illness, BBC Sport reported on 7 November 2026. Rybakina went undefeated on her way to winning the WTA Finals for the first time in 2025.

Source: BBC Sport, “World number one Rybakina withdraws from WTA Finals”, 7 November 2026.

FINAL · OPENING MATCH

Noskova Beats Kostyuk 6-2, 6-4 in the WTA Finals Opener

Linda Noskova won the opening group-stage match on Court 2, beating Marta Kostyuk 6-2, 6-4.

FINAL · ALL-AMERICAN

Gauff Beats Pegula in Three Sets

Coco Gauff beat Jessica Pegula 6-4, 6-7(5), 6-2 in their group-stage match on Court 2. Gauff won the first set 6-4, Pegula took the second in a tiebreak, and Gauff closed out the decider 6-2.

TUESDAY · NOV 10

Sabalenka Takes On Muchová as Day Two Begins

Aryna Sabalenka and Karolina Muchová open Tuesday’s group-stage schedule on Court 2 at 12:00 AM UAE.

TUESDAY · NOV 10

Andreeva Meets Svitolina on Court 2

Mirra Andreeva faces Elina Svitolina after Sabalenka–Muchová, at an estimated 1:10 AM UAE.
