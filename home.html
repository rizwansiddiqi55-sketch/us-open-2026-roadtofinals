Tennis warm-up & injury prevention

A few minutes before and after you play lowers the chance of strains.

⏰

Before you play

8–10 minutes of dynamic moves: leg swings, lunges with a twist, arm circles, then easy rallies.

🛁

After you play

Walk, then hold each stretch 20–30 seconds: shoulders, wrists, quads, hamstrings, calves, hips.

💪

Protect elbow and shoulder

Strengthen the forearm and rotator cuff, and build up serves gradually.

🌞

Heat in the UAE

Play early or late, drink before and during play, and stop if you feel dizzy.
