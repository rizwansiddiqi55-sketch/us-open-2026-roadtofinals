NEWS · 24 NOVEMBER 2026

Sinner has knee surgery; Australian Open in doubt

Jannik Sinner has had “minor” surgery on his troublesome right knee, an operation on the iliotibial band after other treatment did not work, according to reports. Recovery is expected to take about three months, which puts his start at the Australian Open (from 10 January) in doubt. He has not played since winning Wimbledon in July.

Reports: ESPN · Sky Sports · NBC Sports. Recovery time and return date are not confirmed by Sinner.

Fitness tips, warm-up & injury prevention

A few minutes before and after you play lowers the chance of strains.

⏰

Before you play

8–10 minutes of dynamic moves: leg swings, lunges with a twist, arm circles, then easy rallies.

🛁

After you play

Walk, then hold each stretch 20–30 seconds: shoulders, wrists, quads, hamstrings, calves, hips.

💪

Protect elbow and shoulder

Strengthen the forearm and rotator cuff, and build up serves gradually.

🌞

Heat in the UAE

Play early or late, drink before and during play, and stop if you feel dizzy.
