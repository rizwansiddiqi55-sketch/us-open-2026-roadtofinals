About

Last updated: 18 November 2026

TennisByte is an independent fan site for live scores, match recaps and storylines from the big tennis events. It currently covers the Nitto ATP Finals 2026 in Turin, and keeps an archive of the WTA Finals 2026 in Indian Wells (champion: Mirra Andreeva) and the Rolex Paris Masters 2026.

What you get

  • Live scores with set scores, game points and the server
  • Group standings, schedule in Dubai (UAE) time and a simple win predictor
  • Short match stories written from the results
  • Where to watch, with the free options marked

Where the information comes from

Scores and schedules come from ESPN’s public scoreboard, with live game points from a live score feed and match statistics when available. They can run a minute or two behind the court. Player photos come from Wikimedia Commons under free licences, credited on the pages and in players/CREDITS.md.

Independence

This site is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by the ATP, the WTA, any tournament or any player. Names are used only to describe events. We do not stream matches.

The site is run from Dubai, UAE by Rizwan Siddiqi. Corrections are welcome through the contact page.
