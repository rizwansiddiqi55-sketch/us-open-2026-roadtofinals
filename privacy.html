Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

This policy explains what the Tennis Recap website collects and how it is used. We keep it short because we collect very little.

What we collect

  • No accounts, no forms. You do not sign up and we do not ask for your name or email to use the site.
  • Server logs. Our hosting provider, Vercel, processes your IP address and browser details to deliver pages and protect the service. We do not use these logs to identify you.
  • Data saved on your device. The site stores a few items in your browser (local storage and a cache for the installable app): the last scores and match statistics shown, so they are available if the feed is briefly down. This stays on your device and you can clear it in your browser settings.

Third-party services

  • Google Fonts loads the page fonts, so your browser contacts Google, which receives your IP address.
  • Score and statistics providers (ESPN, a live score feed and Cito) are contacted by our server, not by your browser. They do not receive your details.
  • Wikimedia Commons is the source of player photos, which we host ourselves.

Cookies and advertising

We do not currently run advertising or analytics. If we add advertising in future, such as Google AdSense, the ad provider and its partners may use cookies or similar technology to show and measure ads, including personalised ads where the law allows. Google’s use of advertising cookies lets it and its partners serve ads based on visits to this and other sites. You can manage personalised ads at adssettings.google.com or aboutads.info. Where required (for example in the EU and UK), we will ask for your consent through a cookie notice before ads use cookies, and update this page.

Links and sharing

The pages link to other websites, such as broadcasters. We are not responsible for their privacy practices. The Share button uses your device’s own share menu or copies a link; nothing is sent to us.

Children

The site is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Your choices

You can clear saved data in your browser, block cookies, or uninstall the app at any time. If you have questions or requests about your data, use the contact page.

Changes

If we change this policy, the date above will change.
